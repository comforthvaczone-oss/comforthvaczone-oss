Industrial Exhaust Fans

Heavy-duty wall and roof-mounted exhaust fans that remove hot air, fumes and humidity from factory floors, warehouses and workshops.

  • Wall, roof and duct-mounted options
  • Sized for your airflow (CFM) and shed volume
  • Rugged build for continuous duty

Axial Flow Fans

High-volume, low-pressure fans that move air in a straight line through ducts, tunnels and open spaces. Efficient for ventilation, cooling and general air circulation.

  • Jet air and bifurcated axial flow models
  • Suited to transformer, panel and plant cooling
  • Low energy use for the air volume moved

Air Scrubbers

Pollution-control units that wash dust, fumes and harmful gases out of exhaust air before release, helping your plant meet emission norms.

  • Handles dust, fumes and process gases
  • Custom-built to your process and exhaust volume
  • Helps with pollution-board compliance
